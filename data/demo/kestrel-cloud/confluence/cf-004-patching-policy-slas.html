IT Operations : Patching Policy and SLAs

Created by Endpoint Engineering, last modified on Sep 15, 2026

Scope

Operating systems and third-party applications on Kestrel laptops (Macs via Jamf Pro, Windows via Intune), meeting room devices and iPhones. Servers and cloud workloads follow the Platform SRE patching standard.

Remediation SLAs

Measured from vendor release (or from public disclosure if no fix exists) to 95% of active devices patched.

macOS

  • Minor and security updates: Jamf Managed Software Update plan, pilot group SG-Mac-Pilot on day 0, all Macs on day 3, install deadline day 14 (day 7 or sooner for exploited issues).
  • Rapid Security Responses / background security improvements: allowed immediately, no deferral.
  • Major upgrades: deferred up to 90 days with forceDelayedMajorSoftwareUpdates, then enforced in office waves with a 14-day deadline (RB-MAC-003).
  • Supported versions: current major and N-1. N-2 devices are non-compliant 30 days after the new major is enforced.

Feature updates are managed through an Intune feature update policy pinned to the approved Windows 11 version and moved after Endpoint Engineering sign-off. Expedited quality updates are used for exploited vulnerabilities.

Third-party applications

  • Mac: Jamf App Installers and patch management titles for browsers, Slack, Figma and developer tools. Browsers auto-update; a relaunch is forced after 48 hours.
  • Windows: Enterprise App Catalog apps and Win32 supersedence in Intune.
  • Apps without an update path for 30 days past SLA are removed or blocked after notice.

Exceptions

A device that cannot be patched (hardware tied to lab equipment, app incompatibility) needs a security exception ticket approved by Security Operations, with compensating controls (network isolation, no MeshGate) and an end date.

Reporting

Weekly patch compliance report from Defender vulnerability management and the Jamf/Intune dashboards, reviewed in the Monday IT operations meeting. Breach of SLA for a Critical issue is raised as a SEV3 (CF-005).
