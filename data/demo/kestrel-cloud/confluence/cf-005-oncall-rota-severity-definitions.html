IT Operations : IT On-call Rota Rules and Severity Definitions (SEV1-SEV4)

Created by Platform SRE, last modified on Jun 20, 2026

Rota

  • Primary IT on-call covers out-of-hours for workplace systems: identity, endpoints, Google Workspace, MeshGate and office networks. Weekly rotation, handover Monday 10:00 CET (template OC-002).
  • Two follow-the-sun shifts: EU primary 19:00 to 07:00 CET and weekends; US primary covers 18:00 to 23:00 US Central to bridge the gap. During business hours, team queues handle incidents.
  • Secondary on-call is the team lead of the system owner (escalation matrix OC-001).
  • Security Operations runs a separate 24/7 rota for security incidents.
  • No one is on call more than one week in four. Swaps are arranged in #it-oncall and updated in the paging schedule by the person giving away the shift.
  • On-call engineers must be able to reach a laptop and network within 15 minutes and stay sober and fit to work.

When unsure between two severities, pick the higher one. Downgrading later is free; a late upgrade is not.

Incident roles (SEV1 and SEV2)

  • Incident Commander (IC): the on-call engineer until handed over. Coordinates, does not fix.
  • Communications lead: posts in #it-status and the status page. Service Desk lead during business hours.
  • Subject matter experts: paged by the IC from OC-001.
  • First actions: OC-003 (first 15 minutes of a SEV1).

After the incident

  • SEV1 and SEV2: blameless post-mortem (PM-) within 5 business days, reviewed in the weekly ops meeting.
  • SEV3: short summary on the INC ticket.
  • Time worked out of hours is logged for compensation per the local on-call agreement.

Paging rules

  • Pages come only from: the Service Desk out-of-hours phone line, monitoring alerts, and Security Operations.
  • Slack messages are not pages. Anyone needing urgent help out of hours calls the Service Desk number (KB-0014).
