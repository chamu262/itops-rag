# Check your Mac is up to date

Keeping macOS updated closes security holes. Kestrel Macs that fall behind lose access to MeshGate and some apps
(you may see `MG-302` or a "device not compliant" message, KB-0010).

## Check your version

1. Click the Apple menu > **About This Mac**. The macOS version is shown, for example **macOS 26.6.1** or **27.0.1**.
2. Or open **System Settings > General > Software Update**. It says "Your Mac is up to date" or lists an update.

## Install an update

1. Save your work and plug in the charger.
2. Open **System Settings > General > Software Update**.
3. Click **Update Now** (minor update) or **Upgrade Now** (new major version). Enter your password if asked.
4. Your Mac restarts. Minor updates take 15 to 30 minutes; a major upgrade can take an hour.

Updates are downloaded in the background before you see the notification, so installation is usually quick.

## Deadlines

IT sets an install deadline for each update. You get notifications that become more frequent as the deadline
approaches. **At the deadline your Mac restarts and installs the update automatically**, with a 60-minute warning.
Install before then at a time that suits you.

Typical timing at Kestrel:

- **Minor and security updates** (for example 26.6 to 26.6.1): deadline 14 days after release, 7 days or less for
  urgent security fixes.
- **Major upgrades** (for example macOS 26 to macOS 27): you will not see these until IT has tested them, usually 4 to
  8 weeks after Apple's release. Then you have 14 days. Announcements are posted in `#it-announcements`.

## Not enough space

Updates need about 25 GB free. Check with Apple menu > **About This Mac > More Info > Storage**. To free space:

- Self Service > **Storage cleanup**.
- Empty the Trash and the Downloads folder.
- Remove large files you have already stored in Google Drive (Drive for desktop: "Stream files" mode saves space).

## Update stuck

- "Preparing update" for more than an hour: restart the Mac and try again.
- The update is not offered although colleagues have it: open Self Service and run **Update Inventory**, wait 10
  minutes and check again. Your Mac may be in a later rollout wave.
- Error "An error occurred while installing the selected updates": raise a ticket with a screenshot.

## Apps

Self Service updates most apps automatically. Browser updates: Chrome and Safari restart prompts should be accepted the
same day.
