# Meetings without Zoom: using Google Meet rooms

Kestrel uses **Google Meet** for all video meetings. Zoom is not installed on Kestrel laptops and Zoom licences are not
provided. Every meeting room has Google Meet hardware (camera, speaker bar, touch controller).

## Book a room

1. In Google Calendar create an event and click **Rooms**.
2. Filter by office (Stockholm, London, Austin, Lisbon) and capacity. Room names follow the pattern
   `STO-5-Orion (8) [Meet]`: office, floor, name, seats, and whether Meet hardware is present.
3. A Google Meet link is added to the invite automatically.

Rooms release automatically if no one checks in on the room panel within 10 minutes of the start.

## Start the meeting in the room

- Tap the meeting on the room's touch controller and press **Join**. That's it.
- If the meeting is not listed (for example you joined someone else's external link), tap **Join with a code** and
  enter the code from the link.

## Join from your laptop while in the room

If you also join from your laptop in the same room, use **Companion mode** (choose "Use Companion mode" on the join
screen). Your audio stays muted so there is no echo, and you can still chat, raise your hand and share your screen.

## Present from your laptop

- From your laptop in Companion mode click **Present now**, or
- Use the HDMI or USB-C cable on the table; the room shows your screen and shares it into the meeting.

## Meetings set up with Zoom or Teams by external parties

- **Microsoft Teams**: Kestrel rooms can join Teams meetings directly. The room's calendar shows a Teams button when
  the invite contains a Teams link.
- **Zoom**: join from your laptop in the browser (`Join from your browser` link on the Zoom page). You do not need to
  install the Zoom app. Rooms do not support Zoom calls.
- **Webex**: join from the browser.

## Recording and transcripts

Recording and transcripts are available to the meeting organiser. Recordings are saved to the organiser's Drive in a
**Meet Recordings** folder. Tell participants before recording; external participants see a recording notice
automatically.

## Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Room screen black | Tap the controller to wake it. Check the TV input is on HDMI 1. |
| Others cannot hear the room | Check the mute button on the controller and that the speaker bar light is white, not red. |
| Echo | Someone in the room has joined with laptop audio. Use Companion mode. |
| Room shows "Offline" | Report in `#it-help` with the room name. Use a nearby room. |
