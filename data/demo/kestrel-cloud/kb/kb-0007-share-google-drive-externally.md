# Share a Google Drive folder with people outside Kestrel

You can share files with customers, partners and suppliers from Google Drive. The rules below keep customer and
company data safe.

## What the policy allows

| Data | External sharing |
|---|---|
| Public or marketing material | Allowed with named people. "Anyone with the link" allowed for published material only. |
| Internal (most work documents) | Allowed with named people at an organisation we have a contract with. |
| Confidential (customer data, contracts, HR, finance) | Only from a **shared drive** labelled "Confidential - External", to named people, with an expiry date. |
| Restricted (credentials, security reports, personal ID documents) | Never shared externally from Drive. |

"Anyone with the link" sharing is blocked on Kestrel shared drives except the `Marketing - Published` drive.

## Share with a named person

1. Right-click the file or folder > **Share**.
2. Type the person's email address. You see a yellow banner "This person is outside your organisation". That is
   expected.
3. Choose **Viewer** or **Commenter** unless they must edit. Untick **Notify people** only if you will send the link
   yourself.
4. For folders and confidential files, click the person's role > **Add expiration** and set 90 days or less.
5. Click **Share**.

## Share a whole project with a partner

Ask for a shared drive instead of sharing a My Drive folder. Raise a request in the Service Desk portal: "New shared
drive". Give the name, Kestrel managers (at least two) and the external domain. A shared drive keeps files when
people leave Kestrel, and access can be reviewed in one place.

## Restrictions you might see

**"Sharing outside Kestrel is not allowed for this item"**
The file has a Confidential or Restricted label, or is on a shared drive that blocks external sharing. Move the file to
an appropriate drive or ask the shared drive manager.

**"Your administrator does not allow sharing with this domain"**
Some domains (personal email providers like gmail.com for confidential drives) are blocked. Use the recipient's work
address.

**External user cannot open the file**
They must sign in with a Google account that matches the address you shared with. If they have no Google account,
they can verify with a one-time PIN sent to their email (visitor sharing).

## Reviewing access

Each quarter you receive an email listing files you own that are shared externally. Remove access you no longer
need: **Share > (person) > Remove access**.

## Do not

- Do not download customer data and email it as an attachment as a workaround for a blocked share.
- Do not use personal cloud storage (personal Dropbox, WeTransfer) for Kestrel data.
